Scouty limits some searches, AI actions, and email activity to keep the service reliable. The allowance can vary, so use the remaining limit shown in the app as the current value.

## Research limits

Actions such as **Check matches**, finding companies, finding decision makers, and looking up work email use your workspace's research allowance. Everyone in the workspace, and any [connected AI app](/ai-apps/connect), shares it.

When an allowance is used, Scouty shows a message such as "Your share of today's research allowance is used. New research resumes with the next daily allowance." You can continue reviewing results that are already saved.

A saved campaign also has daily limits for new companies and contacts. When one is reached, the campaign's **Progress** tab shows the limit and the time and time zone at which work resumes. Daily campaign limits reset at the start of the next day in the campaign's time zone. Contacts already queued keep moving.

## Sending and billing

Sent email uses trial credit, then pay-as-you-go. When trial credit is used up or a payment fails, new sending pauses. Research, drafts, and replies stay available. See [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing).

## Saved results

Scouty saves your progress and company results automatically. You can reload the page or return later to continue where you left off.

- More company results load when needed. Returning to an earlier page reuses loaded results.
- The result date tells you when the company information was found.
- After changing targeting, review the match count and results again.
- To find a different set of companies, create or select a campaign with different targeting.

## Common problems

### A research allowance is used

Wait until the time shown, then retry. Continue reviewing saved results. Repeatedly selecting **Check matches** does not bypass the limit.

### A campaign is not sending

Open the campaign and read the **Auto-send** status and the **Progress** tab. Common causes are a paused campaign, a time outside the sending hours, a missing sending mailbox, used-up trial credit, or a workspace set to drafts only. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation#read-the-status).

### An email was sent that you did not expect

A saved campaign sends on its own. Select **Pause** on the campaign, then review its targeting, [writing prompt](/email/writing-prompt), and sending hours. A sent email cannot be recalled.

### Company research fails

Check the website spelling and make sure the public website opens. Retry the step. Use the business form when no public website exists. If another company analysis is still running, wait for it to finish before retrying.

### Market must be selected

Select **Not quite right? Edit the details**, choose a specific market or **Global**, and save. Scouty needs this information before it can find relevant competitors.

### A later step disappeared after editing

Changes to the company profile, campaign, or targeting can clear later results that no longer match. Continue from the step you changed. Sent email and saved project history remain available.

### Work email cannot be revealed or verified

Choose another decision maker. Scouty does not send to an unverified address.

### Email sending is not confirmed

Select **Check status** and wait for the status to update. Do not send the same email again while Scouty is checking it.

### Replies are not current

Open the **Inbox** and select **Check for replies**. If the check fails, wait and retry. Do not send another opening email to refresh the conversation.

### Google Calendar connection failed or was canceled

Your saved meeting method is unchanged. Choose **Google Calendar** again in **Meeting setup**. You can continue managing email without it. See [Meeting setup](/meetings/google-calendar#recover-from-connection-problems).

### A setting is unavailable to you

Some settings need a workspace owner or admin, and billing needs the owner. See [roles](/settings/people#roles).

### An AI app cannot use Scouty

See [troubleshooting a connected app](/ai-apps/connect#troubleshooting).

### Custom domain remains pending

Compare every DNS **Type**, **Name**, **Value**, **Proxy status**, and **Priority** with the table. Wait for the changes to appear, then select **Check connection**. Ask the person who manages your domain for help if needed. See [Custom domain](/settings/custom-domain#wait-for-dns-propagation).

## Continue where you left off

Reload the current page first. Scouty restores completed work when it is available. Use the stepper to review the six research steps. Use the **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Emails** tabs to move between results. Open a saved campaign from the Dashboard to resume it.

If you changed the account, company, campaign, or input while an action was running, review the currently displayed item and its status first. Retry only when the UI offers it. Do not repeat a send whose result is still being confirmed.

## Information to collect for support

Email [contact@scouty.to](mailto:contact@scouty.to). Include:

- the workspace, page, and campaign name;
- the action and button you used;
- the approximate time and your time zone;
- the visible error text and status;
- whether retrying or reloading changed the result;
- a screenshot with personal information hidden.

Never share passwords, access keys, account credentials, or private sign-in links.

## Opening a campaign fails

Use **Try again** or return to the Dashboard. Sign in to the account that owns the campaign if requested. A loading error does not mean you should create another campaign or analyze the website again.

## Website cannot be changed

A workspace is linked to one website. Archiving does not let you replace it. An owner or admin can permanently delete the archived site and then choose another website. Deleting removes the site's campaigns, conversations, and meetings. See [your website and campaigns](/getting-started/accounts-and-projects#change-your-website).

Scouty emails you when something needs your attention and sends a regular progress summary. Everyone in a workspace gets these by default.

Open **Settings → Notifications**. Your choices are personal. They apply to you across all your workspaces and do not change what teammates receive. Changes save as you make them.

## Pause everything

Turn off **Send me product notification emails** to pause instant alerts and progress summaries. Your other choices are kept for when you turn it back on.

## Instant alerts

Each alert is one email sent when the event happens. All are on by default.

| Alert | When it is sent |
| --- | --- |
| **Prospect replies** | A prospect replies. The email says whether they want to meet, have a question, or are not interested. Out-of-office replies are skipped. |
| **Campaign started and finished** | A campaign goes live, and a recap when it has reached every prospect. |
| **Meetings** | A meeting is booked, or a booking needs your attention. |
| **Automation attention** | An automation needs your help. |
| **First leads ready** | A campaign first has outreach ready to review. |

A reply alert can arrive a few minutes after the reply while Scouty works out what the prospect wants.

## Progress summary

The summary is one digest of leads ready, emails sent, replies received, and meetings booked across all your workspaces. It is skipped when nothing changed.

Choose **How often**:

- **Daily at 9 a.m.** (default)
- **Weekly on Monday at 9 a.m.**
- **Off**

## Time zone

**Email timezone** sets the 9 a.m. delivery time and the date range of your summary. Enter a time zone name such as `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` or `Europe/Berlin`, then select **Save timezone**.

Scouty uses your browser's time zone the first time you open the page. Times use UTC until a time zone is saved.

## Emails you always receive

Account, billing, and security emails are always sent. Examples include a failed payment, a newly connected app, a workspace invitation, and a change to your workspace access.

## Unsubscribe from an email

The link at the bottom of a notification email opens **Turn off notifications?**. Selecting **Turn off notifications** pauses all optional emails, the same as the switch on this page. Your individual choices are kept. Use **Manage email settings** to turn them back on.

## If settings do not save

Scouty restores the previous value and shows an error. Select **Try again**, or reload the page. If the time zone is rejected, check its spelling.

A workspace is the team space that holds your website, campaigns, inbox, sending mailbox, and usage allowance. Everyone in the workspace shares them.

Open **Settings → People** to manage who can work in the workspace.

## Roles

Each workspace has one owner.

| Role | What they can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Full control, including billing, promoting admins, and transferring ownership. |
| **Admin** | Everything a member can do, plus inviting and removing members, workspace settings, and integrations. |
| **Member** | Research, campaigns, the shared inbox, and automations using the workspace allowance. |

Some settings need a particular role:

| Task | Who can do it |
| --- | --- |
| Change [billing](/settings/billing) and payment details | Owner |
| Promote or demote admins, transfer ownership | Owner |
| Invite and remove members, rename the workspace | Owner and admin |
| Set up the website and the shared sending mailbox | Owner and admin |
| Manage the [custom domain](/settings/custom-domain) | Owner and admin |
| Connect an [AI app](/ai-apps/connect) | Owner and admin |
| Allow automatic sending for a campaign | Owner and admin |

## Rename the workspace

Owners and admins can change **Workspace name** and select **Save name**. The name appears to teammates, in invitation emails, and in the workspace switcher. It can be up to 80 characters.

## Invite a teammate by email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Select **Invite teammate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the details">
    Enter the **Email address** and choose a **Role**. Only the owner can invite an **Admin**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Select **Send invitation**. Scouty emails the invitation and shows an **Invitation link** once. Share that link only with the person you invited. It works only for that email address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An invitation expires in seven days. Track it under **Pending invitations**, where each one shows its status, expiry date, and whether the email was delivered.

- **Resend** issues a fresh link. The previous link stops working.
- **Revoke** cancels the invitation.
- If the email shows **Not delivered**, check the address and resend.
- Only the owner can resend or revoke an admin invitation.

## Invite with a link

Use a link when you want several people to join as members.

1. Open **Invite links**.
2. Set **Maximum joins**, from 1 to 100.
3. Select **Create link** and copy it. The full link is shown only once.

Anyone with the link and a verified account can join as a member, so share it only with people you trust. Links expire after seven days. **Revoke** a link to stop new joins. People who already joined keep their access.

## Accept an invitation

1. Open the link from the invitation email.
2. Sign in, or create an account, with the email address that received the invitation.
3. Verify your email address if asked.
4. Select **Join workspace**.

If the page says the invitation has expired, is no longer active, or has reached its limit, ask a workspace owner or admin for a new one.

## Switch workspaces

If you belong to more than one workspace, use the **Workspace** switcher at the top of the sidebar. Each option shows the workspace name and your role. Switching returns you to the Dashboard.

Finish or save what you are doing first. Scouty asks before discarding unsaved edits and waits for an action in progress to finish.

## Change a role or remove someone

On **Members**:

- The owner can select **Make admin** or **Make member**.
- **Remove** takes away access immediately. The person's existing work and approved automations stay in the workspace. The owner can remove anyone else. Admins can remove members.

Scouty emails the person when their role or access changes.

## Leave a workspace

Select **Leave workspace** under **Leave this workspace**. Campaigns, emails, and automations you created stay with the team. You need a new invitation to return.

The owner cannot leave. Transfer ownership first.

## Transfer ownership

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the dialog">
    Select **Transfer ownership**. The workspace needs at least two people.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the new owner">
    Choose a teammate under **New owner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Type the workspace name, then confirm with your password or your Google account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The new owner controls the workspace and its billing. You become an admin. The mailbox, history, and allowance stay with the workspace.

## Common messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This person is already a workspace member." | No invitation is needed. |
| "An invitation is already pending. Resend it from Pending invitations." | Resend the existing invitation instead of creating another. |
| "Sign in with the email address this invitation was sent to." | Use **Use a different account** and sign in with the invited address. |
| "You no longer have access to" a workspace | An owner or admin removed you. Ask them to invite you again. |
| "You're not in a workspace yet" | Open your invitation link, or ask an owner or admin for a new invitation. |

Scouty charges for sent email only. Research, drafts, and replies are free. There is no monthly subscription fee.

Open **Settings → Billing & Usage** to see your usage, payment method, and invoices.

## What you pay for

| Item | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| A sent opening email or scheduled follow-up | $0.03 per email ($30 per 1,000 emails) |
| Research, drafts, and replies | Free |
| An email that failed to send | Not counted |
| An email that was sent and later bounced | Counted |

## Trial credit

Each account gets one $10 trial. It has no expiry and needs no card. Trial credit pays for sent email at the same price.

When the trial is used up, Scouty stops new sending and shows "Your trial credit is used up. Add a payment method to keep sending." Campaign automation and follow-ups wait until billing is set up. You can keep researching, drafting, and replying.

## Enable pay-as-you-go

Only the workspace owner can change billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing setup">
    Select **Enable pay-as-you-go** on the **Billing & Usage** page, or **Add billing** on the Dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree and add a card">
    Tick the consent box, then enter your card in the secure checkout. Payment details are handled by Stripe. Nothing is charged today, and remaining trial credit goes toward future usage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resume sending">
    Saving a card does not send anything. Select **Resume sending** to continue previously approved campaigns and scheduled follow-ups. For a single email, return to its draft and send it again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Usage is billed monthly, with no base fee and no default spending cap. Control volume with each campaign's daily limits. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation).

## Read the page

| Item | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Trial credit remaining** | Unused trial credit. |
| **Emails this billing period** | Opening emails and scheduled follow-ups sent in the current period. |
| **Estimated usage charges** | The estimate before applicable taxes and invoice adjustments. |
| **Next billing date** | When the current monthly period is billed. The period starts when billing is activated. |
| **Payment method** | The saved card, or **No payment method saved**. |

Emails still awaiting send confirmation are listed separately and are left out of the estimate until confirmed.

Invoices are listed with **Invoice**, **Date**, **Total**, and **Status**. Select an invoice number to open it. Small invoice balances may carry forward until payment can be collected. Use **Manage payment details** to change your card.

## Pause or resume paid sending

Select **Stop paid sending** to pause future billable email. Messages already submitted may still send, and usage already incurred remains payable. Research, drafts, and replies stay available.

Select **Resume sending** when you want campaigns and follow-ups to continue.

## If a payment fails

Scouty pauses new sending until the payment is resolved. Research, drafts, and replies are still available. The owner selects **Update payment** or **Resolve payment**, fixes the card, and then selects **Resume sending**.

## Who can manage billing

| Role | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | View usage and change billing, payment details, and paid sending. |
| Admin and member | View the page. They see "Only the workspace owner can change billing and payment settings." |

## Workspaces billed by invoice

Some workspaces are invoiced directly by arrangement with Scouty. They show **Billed by invoice** and need no card or online payment. The page shows usage counted by calendar month in UTC. Sending is not limited by trial credit or a payment method. Contact Scouty with questions about an invoice.

## Troubleshooting

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Your trial credit is used up. Open Billing & Usage to continue automation." | Ask the owner to enable pay-as-you-go and select **Resume sending**. |
| "Sending paused · Update your payment method" | The owner updates the card, then resumes sending. |
| "Billing ready · Resume paid sending when you're ready" | The card is saved. Select **Resume sending**. |
| Billing setup is still confirming | You can close the window and return later. Select **Check status**. |
| "Secure checkout couldn't load." | Check your connection and try again. Your campaign is saved. |
| "Pay-as-you-go billing is not enabled yet." | Paid sending is not available for the workspace yet. Your campaign is saved. |
| Payment details and invoices could not be refreshed | Your usage is still shown. Try again shortly. |

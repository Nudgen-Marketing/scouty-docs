**Meeting setup** decides how an interested prospect books time with you. It belongs to one site. Only one method is active at a time.

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Booking link** | Prospects choose a time on your own scheduling page, such as a Google Appointment Schedule or Calendly page. |
| **Google Calendar** | Scouty books meetings from replies using your calendar, or drafts alternative times for your review. |

## Open Meeting setup

Select your website in the Dashboard sidebar and find **Meeting setup** under **Site settings**.

## Use a booking link

1. Choose **Booking link**.
2. Enter the full link under **HTTPS scheduling URL**. It must begin with `https://`.
3. Select **Save meeting setup**.

The link is shared only after a lead shows clear meeting interest. It is never included in first emails or unanswered follow-ups. A booking made on your scheduling page does not update the conversation in Scouty.

## Use Google Calendar

1. Choose **Google Calendar**.
2. Select **Connect Google Calendar** and choose the Google account for this site.
3. Review the requested permissions and approve the connection.
4. Back in Scouty, choose the **Meeting length**: 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes.
5. Decide whether to tick **Book meetings automatically when they confirm an available time**.
6. Select **Save meeting setup**.

You can keep managing email without meeting setup and return to it later.

### Automatic booking

When automatic booking is on, Scouty can book a time that a reply explicitly confirms. The time must be on a weekday between 07:00 and 21:00 in the connected calendar's time zone, and the calendar must be free.

If the time is unavailable, or the reply does not contain one clear time, Scouty drafts a reply with available options. Suggested replies are saved for your review, not sent automatically.

A booked event appears in the conversation activity and includes a Google Meet link. Cancellation and RSVP changes can update the meeting status.

### Book a time manually

Open the conversation and use the booking form when you and the prospect have agreed on a time, or when automatic booking needs help. Confirm the time carefully. Scouty sends a calendar invitation with a Google Meet link when the booking succeeds.

## Change or turn off the method

- To switch methods, choose the other one and select **Save meeting setup**.
- To use a different Google account, select **Disconnect Google Calendar**, confirm with **Disconnect**, then connect the correct account. New meetings are no longer booked through the old connection. Existing meetings and your saved booking link stay unchanged.
- Select **Reconnect Google Calendar** if the connection stopped working.
- Select **Turn off meeting setup** to stop offering either method.

## Recover from connection problems

- If Google Calendar could not be connected, your saved meeting method is unchanged. Choose **Google Calendar** to try again.
- If settings cannot be saved, reload the saved settings and try again. Your unsaved choices are kept, so review them before saving.
- If **Google Calendar is currently unavailable**, use a booking link or continue managing email and return later.

<Note>
  Meeting setup belongs to one site. Archiving the site stops automatic booking. Existing meetings are not canceled.
</Note>

This guide takes you through the shortest path from your business information to a reviewed email. You need a company website or enough information to describe your business.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Research your company">
    Open [Scouty](https://scouty.to/). Enter your website and select **Find my prospects**. If you do not have a website, use the business form instead.

    Review the profile in **Research your company**. Check the description, **Language**, and **Market**. Select **Not quite right? Edit the details** to correct them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review competitors">
    Select **Explore competitors**. Scouty looks for direct competitors with evidence that they serve your selected market.

    If the market is unknown, choose it in the profile first. Scouty does not broaden an uncertain market to worldwide results automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a campaign">
    Pick a campaign in **Define campaigns**. Review the industries, company sizes, locations, and job titles under **Who we'll search for**.

    Select **Check matches** before continuing. If the result is empty or very broad, edit the targeting and check again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a company and person">
    In **Find potential customers**, select a company after reviewing its fit score and explanation. Use **Show more companies** to load another group when available.

    In **Find decision makers**, select **Add** for a person you want to contact. Revealing a work email uses a search credit unless it was already revealed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and set up the sender">
    Sign in when Scouty asks you to save the work and prepare email. Verify your account if prompted.

    In **Write emails**, choose the sending address and sender name. Scouty creates a project and an editable draft.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and send">
    Read the subject and message. Select **Save changes** after editing, or enter an instruction under **Rewrite with AI** and select **Rewrite**.

    When the address is verified and the draft is ready, select **Send email**, then **Send now**. You cannot edit or recall the message after it is submitted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Prepare for replies">
    After the send is confirmed, Scouty opens **Book meetings**. Select **Connect Google Calendar**, or select **Set up later** to enter the project immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  A visitor who is not signed in can complete one company profile trial in that browser. Reloading or continuing the same profile does not use another trial. Starting a different company requires sign-in.
</Info>

## If a step fails

- Correct an invalid website or incomplete form without losing a trial.
- Use the retry action shown in the step. Scouty does not count a failed analysis as a completed anonymous profile.
- If sending remains on **Confirming your email went out…**, select **Check now**. Scouty checks the mailbox instead of sending the message again.
- Your completed steps are restored when saved data is available. Avoid starting a different company unless you intend to clear later research selections.

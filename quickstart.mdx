---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from your website to a saved campaign that finds prospects and sends reviewed outreach."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your website">
    Open [Scouty](https://scouty.to/), enter your website, and select **Find my prospects**. Selecting a website suggestion also starts the check. Use the business form to explore without a website.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your company profile">
    Scouty continues to competitors and campaign ideas when the market is known. If the market is missing, select **Not quite right? Edit the details**, choose it, and save. Use **Next step** and **Previous step** to move between steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a campaign">
    Pick a suggested campaign, or select **Describe your own campaign**. Review **Who we'll search for**, adjust targeting, and select **Check matches**. Select **Next step** when ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review companies, contacts, and emails">
    Browse **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Emails** as results arrive. Scouty chooses contacts and prepares a few preview emails. Edit a preview until it reads the way you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the campaign">
    Select **Start campaign** and sign in if prompted. Review **Save your campaign**, then select **Confirm website and save**. This links the workspace to that website. A description-only profile needs a real website first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish sender setup">
    Create your sending mailbox if asked. Scouty then opens the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the campaign">
    The campaign now finds contacts, writes emails, and sends them on its own. Use **Pause** in the campaign header at any time. Work through replies in the **Inbox**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Saving a campaign starts automatic research and sending once your mailbox is ready. Sent email uses your $10 trial credit, then pay-as-you-go at $0.03 per sent email. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation) and [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing).
</Warning>

<Info>
  You can research and edit email previews before signing in. Nothing is sent until you save a campaign. Usage limits apply throughout the flow.
</Info>

## Set up the rest

- [Sending hours](/find-prospects/campaign-automation#set-sending-hours) for each campaign.
- [Auto follow-up](/email/auto-follow-up) and [Meeting setup](/meetings/google-calendar) for the site.
- [Knowledge](/find-prospects/knowledge) and a [writing prompt](/email/writing-prompt) to shape what Scouty writes.
- [People](/settings/people) to invite teammates.
- [An AI app](/ai-apps/connect) such as ChatGPT or Claude, to work with Scouty from a chat.

## If you need to stop or retry

- Missing market: edit the profile and save it.
- Failed research or email generation: use the retry action shown for that item.
- A usage limit: wait for the time shown in Scouty.
- Sending or delivery is not confirmed: check its status in the Mailbox before taking another action. Do not create a replacement send.
- Opening a saved campaign fails: use **Try again** or return to the Dashboard. You do not need to start a new website analysis.

See [your website and campaigns](/getting-started/accounts-and-projects) and [draft and send email](/email/drafts-and-sending).

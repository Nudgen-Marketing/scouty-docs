---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Go from your website to reviewed drafts and your project Dashboard."
---

1. Open [Scouty](https://scouty.to/), enter your website, and select **Find my prospects**. Selecting a website suggestion also starts the check. You can select a suggestion with the arrow keys and Enter. Use the business form if you want to explore without a website.
2. Review your company profile. Scouty continues to competitors and campaign ideas when the market is known. If it is missing, select **Not quite right? Edit the details**, choose the market, and save. You can return to earlier steps to review or correct them.
3. Choose a campaign and select **Use this campaign**. Review **Who we'll search for**, adjust targeting, and use **Check matches**. Select **Find contacts** when ready.
4. Browse **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Write emails** as results arrive. Scouty automatically selects up to five contacts, choosing the best available match at each company. Use **Draft email** to choose more people.
5. Review and edit an email preview. Select **Save & continue** when ready to save your work. Sign in if prompted, then review **Confirm website and save**. This permanently links the account to that website. A description-only profile needs a real website before confirmation.
6. Complete sender setup if requested, save your draft edits, and select **Open dashboard**. Open a draft there to review **From**, **To**, **Cc**, the subject, and message. Select **Send email**, then confirm with **Send now**.
7. Manage replies, [follow-ups](/email/auto-follow-up), and [Calendar](/meetings/google-calendar) from the project Dashboard.

<Info>
You can research and edit email previews before signing in. Saving a campaign is separate from sending email. Usage limits apply throughout the flow.
</Info>

## If you need to stop or retry

- Missing market: edit the profile and save it.
- Failed research or email generation: use the retry action shown for that item.
- A usage limit: wait for the reset or retry time shown in Scouty.
- Sending or delivery is not confirmed: check its status in the Dashboard before taking another action. Do not create a replacement send.
- Opening a saved campaign fails: use **Try again** or return to the Dashboard. You do not need to start a new website analysis.

See [accounts and saved work](/getting-started/accounts-and-projects) and [drafts and sending](/email/drafts-and-sending).

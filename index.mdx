Scouty helps you find companies and decision makers, prepare personal email, and turn replies into meetings. Start with a website or describe your business in a form.

## The six-step research workflow

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1. **Research your company** | Review the profile, content language, and market. |
| 2. **Explore competitors** | Review relevant competitors in your market. |
| 3. **Define campaigns** | Choose an audience and confirm targeting. |
| 4. **Find potential customers** | Browse company matches. |
| 5. **Find decision makers** | Review contacts and select people for email drafts. |
| 6. **Write emails** | Review previews, save your campaign, and edit drafts. |

Research advances through the first three steps when current results are ready and the market is known. Returning to an earlier step lets you review it without forcing you forward.

The wizard ends at **Write emails**. Use **Open dashboard** for saved campaigns to send email, manage replies, configure follow-ups, and connect Calendar. Finishing research does not send email or book a meeting.

## Your website and campaigns

Your account is linked to one fixed website. Its saved workspace is called a project. That project can contain multiple campaigns, each with its own targeting and results. Booking link, Calendar, and follow-up settings belong to the site; mailbox and Custom domain settings belong to the account.

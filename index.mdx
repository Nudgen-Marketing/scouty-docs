Scouty is a guided prospecting and outreach workspace. Give it your company website or describe your business in a form. Scouty researches your market, builds campaigns, finds companies and decision makers, drafts personal email, and helps you turn replies into meetings.

## Who Scouty is for

Scouty works best for founders, small sales teams, agencies, and service businesses that want focused outreach without building a complex sales stack. You stay in control of targeting, recipients, email content, follow-ups, and calendar settings.

## The eight-step workflow

| Step | What you do | What Scouty keeps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Research your company** | Confirm what you sell, the content language, and the markets you serve. | A company profile you can correct. |
| 2. **Explore competitors** | Review direct competitors that serve the selected market. | Evidence-backed competitor research. |
| 3. **Define campaigns** | Choose a campaign and refine targeting. | A versioned campaign brief. |
| 4. **Find potential customers** | Review company leads, fit scores, and reasons. | An automatic snapshot of each loaded page. |
| 5. **Find decision makers** | Pick people at a company and reveal verified work email. | Selected contacts for the campaign. |
| 6. **Write emails** | Set a sending address and review each draft. | Editable drafts inside a project. |
| 7. **Send emails** | Confirm the message and track delivery or replies. | The conversation and delivery history. |
| 8. **Book meetings** | Connect Google Calendar or continue to the project. | Calendar settings and meeting activity. |

<Note>
  Scouty does not send an opening email until you press **Send email** and confirm **Send now**. AI-generated drafts remain editable before sending.
</Note>

## Projects and campaigns

A **campaign** is a targeting strategy for one audience. A **project** is the saved workspace that contains outreach, conversations, follow-ups, and meeting activity. One project can keep the campaign context that produced its emails.

After an email is sent, open its project from the Dashboard to monitor replies, prepare follow-ups, manage the booking link, and connect Google Calendar.

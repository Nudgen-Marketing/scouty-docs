Scouty helps you find companies and decision makers, prepare personal email, and turn replies into meetings. Start with a website or describe your business in a form.

## The six-step research workflow

| Step | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| 1. **Research your company** | Review the profile, content language, and market. |
| 2. **Explore competitors** | Review relevant competitors in your market. |
| 3. **Define campaigns** | Choose an audience and confirm targeting. |
| 4. **Find potential customers** | Browse company matches. |
| 5. **Find decision makers** | Review contacts and select people for email drafts. |
| 6. **Write emails** | Review previews, save your campaign, and edit drafts. |

Research advances through the first three steps when current results are ready and the market is known. Returning to an earlier step lets you review it without forcing you forward.

You can explore every step before signing in. Nothing is sent until you save a campaign. A saved campaign then keeps finding contacts, writing emails, and sending them on its own. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation).

## Your workspace, website, and campaigns

A workspace is your team's shared space. It is linked to one website, and the saved work for that website is called a project or site. The site can contain multiple campaigns, each with its own targeting and results.

| Where | What you manage there |
| --- | --- |
| **Mailbox** | **Inbox**, **Sent**, and **Drafts** for the whole workspace. |
| **Email analytics** | Delivery results and estimated engagement. |
| **Your website** | **Knowledge**, **Campaigns**, and site settings for follow-ups, meetings, and sender details. |
| **Settings** | **People**, **Billing & Usage**, **Connected Apps**, **Notifications**, and **Custom domain**. |

Short tours introduce each area the first time you open it.

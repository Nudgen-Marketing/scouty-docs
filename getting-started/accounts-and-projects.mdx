## Explore before saving

You can research multiple companies and edit email previews before signing in. Research does not confirm a website, send email, or book a meeting. Use **Research a different company** in a new research flow to change the business being explored.

Select **Save & continue** when you want to save a campaign. Sign in and verify your email if prompted. Closing the sign-in dialog lets you continue reviewing your work.

## Confirm your website

Review **Confirm website and save** carefully. Each account is permanently linked to one real website. Once confirmed, you can save more campaigns for that website. If it is already linked to your account, use **Save & continue**.

A business-form profile supports research, but you must research a real website before confirming it. An account already linked to another website cannot switch to the new one. Use another account for that website.

<Warning>
Archiving does not release the website link or let you replace it. Permanent deletion of archived sites is not available.
</Warning>

## Work with campaigns

The site workspace is also called a project. Its **Campaigns** section contains saved campaigns.

- **Add campaign** starts another audience for the same site.
- **Open campaign** resumes that campaign's targeting, results, and drafts. It does not create a duplicate campaign.
- Unsaved campaign suggestions are ideas; they are not automatically saved as campaigns.
- A saved campaign does not offer **Research a different company** in step 1.
- Booking link, Calendar, and follow-up settings belong to the site. Custom domain and mailbox settings are shared by the account.

Opening a saved campaign reuses valid results. Unfinished research may continue when eligible; opening the campaign does not automatically send email. Use **Open dashboard** from **Write emails** to manage outreach.

## Archive and restore

Use **Archive site** in the site's menu to pause its activity while keeping its history. Archived sites appear under **Archived sites** and are read-only. Pending follow-ups and automatic booking stop. Existing email and external meetings are not deleted or canceled.

If an email or meeting action is still being confirmed, Scouty may ask you to wait before archiving. Review its status instead of assuming it failed.

Use **Restore site** for your fixed website when available. Restoring does not restart stopped follow-up sequences or automatically enable automation.

Accounts with older projects for several websites must choose one website to keep. The other sites remain historical and read-only; they cannot be restored as replacement websites.

## Continue saved work

Open the campaign from the Dashboard or reload its page. Saved campaign data is available after signing in on another device. Save important draft edits before leaving; unsaved previews and edits should not be treated as saved account data.

Older progress from the former sending or meeting steps opens at a valid step in the six-step wizard. Delivery and meetings are managed on the Dashboard.

Changing research or targeting can invalidate later in-progress results. Review the new results before continuing. Existing saved campaign history remains available. If opening a campaign fails, use **Try again** or return to the Dashboard.

See [Auto GTM](/find-prospects/auto-gtm) for ongoing automation and [drafts and sending](/email/drafts-and-sending) for reviewing outbound email.

You can research several companies before creating an account. Sign in when you are ready to create a project, prepare outreach, send email, or manage replies and meetings.

## Research before signing in

Start with a website or the business form. You can start another company from the home page or select **Research a different company** in step 1.

- You can review, edit, and continue each company profile before signing in.
- Scouty keeps completed research available when you return in the same browser.
- Only one new company analysis runs at a time. Wait for it to finish before starting another.
- Research and AI usage limits still apply. If you reach a limit, wait for the time shown in Scouty and try again.

<Note>
  Sign in before creating a project or writing and sending email. Signing in also makes it easier to continue your outreach from another device.
</Note>

## Sign in and verify your account

Sign in from the prompt shown when you reach email writing, or use the account menu. When you create an account, open the verification message if Scouty asks you to verify your email. Return to Scouty and use the available check action after completing verification.

## Projects

A project is created when the outreach flow needs to save a sending address and draft. The Dashboard lists your projects and their sent and replied totals.

Open a project to:

- search and filter its email conversations;
- check replies and delivery state;
- configure a [Booking link](/email/drafts-and-sending#add-a-booking-link);
- enable [Auto follow-up](/email/auto-follow-up);
- connect [Google Calendar](/meetings/google-calendar);
- resume prospecting for the same company.

Deleting a project removes its research and unsent drafts. A project with sent email is kept so Scouty can continue tracking its conversation history.

## Progress recovery

Scouty saves your progress automatically so you can continue where you left off. If you revisit an earlier completed step, **Next step: N** moves one step forward without repeating completed work.

Changing your company profile or targeting clears later results that no longer match. Review the updated flow from that point. Saved projects and sent email history remain on the Dashboard.

<Warning>
  Do not use **Research a different company** unless you want to clear the current in-progress research and selections after step 1.
</Warning>

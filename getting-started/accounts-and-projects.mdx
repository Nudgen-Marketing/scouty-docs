## Explore before saving

You can research multiple companies and edit email previews before signing in. Research does not confirm a website, send email, or book a meeting. Use **Research a different company** in a new research flow to change the business being explored.

Select **Start campaign** under an email preview when you want to save a campaign. Sign in and verify your email if prompted. Closing the sign-in dialog lets you continue reviewing your work.

## Confirm your website

Review **Confirm website and save** carefully. Each workspace is linked to one real website. Once confirmed, you can save more campaigns for that website. If it is already linked, the button reads **Save & start campaign**.

Only a workspace owner or admin can set up the website. A business-form profile supports research, but you must research a real website before confirming it.

<Warning>
Saving a campaign starts automatic research and sending once your mailbox is ready. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation).
</Warning>

## Work with campaigns

The site workspace is also called a project. Its **Campaigns** section contains saved campaigns.

- **Add campaign** starts another audience for the same site.
- Select a campaign to open its page, with its audience, leads, and progress. **Open research** returns to the research steps without creating a duplicate campaign.
- Unsaved campaign suggestions are ideas; they are not automatically saved as campaigns.
- A saved campaign does not offer **Research a different company** in step 1.
- **Site settings** hold Auto follow-up, Meeting setup, and sender details for the site. The mailbox, custom domain, billing, and people are shared by the workspace.

Opening a saved campaign reuses valid results. A saved campaign keeps researching and sending on its own unless it is paused. Use **Back to campaign** or **Back to dashboard** to leave the research steps.

## Archive and restore

Use **Archive site** in the site's menu to pause its activity while keeping its history. Archived sites appear under **Archived sites** and are read-only. Pending follow-ups and automatic booking stop. Existing email and external meetings are not deleted or canceled.

If an email or meeting action is still being confirmed, Scouty may ask you to wait before archiving. Review its status instead of assuming it failed.

Use **Restore site** for your website when available. Restoring does not restart stopped follow-up sequences. Automation stays off until you select **Resume** on each campaign.

## Change your website

Archiving alone does not let you choose another website. To change it, an owner or admin archives the site and then selects **Delete site permanently** in the archived site's menu. Type the site's domain to confirm.

<Warning>
Deleting a site cannot be undone. Its campaigns, leads, drafts, conversations, meetings, and automations are deleted. The workspace can then choose another website.
</Warning>

Accounts with older projects for several websites must choose one website to keep. The other sites remain historical and read-only; they cannot be restored as replacement websites.

## Continue saved work

Open the campaign from the Dashboard or reload its page. Saved campaign data is available after signing in on another device. Save important draft edits before leaving; unsaved previews and edits should not be treated as saved account data.

Older progress from the former sending or meeting steps opens at a valid step in the six-step wizard. Delivery and meetings are managed in the Mailbox and site settings.

Changing research or targeting can invalidate later in-progress results. Review the new results before continuing. Existing saved campaign history remains available. If opening a campaign fails, use **Try again** or return to the Dashboard.

See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation) for what a saved campaign does, [People](/settings/people) for roles, and [draft and send email](/email/drafts-and-sending) for reviewing outbound email.

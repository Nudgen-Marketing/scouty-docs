Each campaign has a **Writing prompt** card on its **Campaign** tab. It holds two settings: the writing prompt and the link for replies. Workspace members can edit both unless the site is archived.

## Writing prompt

The writing prompt tells the AI how to write first emails, follow-ups, and replies for this campaign: tone, length, structure, language, and things to avoid. Facts still come from your campaign and [Knowledge](/find-prospects/knowledge).

1. Open the campaign and find **Writing prompt** on the **Campaign** tab. The badge shows **In use** or **Not set**.
2. Type your guidance, or start from an example such as **Short and direct**, **Peer to peer**, or **Formal for enterprise**. The prompt can be up to 2,000 characters.
3. Select **Save writing prompt**. Use **Clear** to remove it.

If someone else changed the prompt while you were editing, select **Reload prompt** and review their version before saving.

You can also set the prompt in the **Write emails** step before you save a campaign.

### What changes after you save

- Emails written from now on follow the new prompt.
- Existing drafts keep their wording until you rewrite them.
- While automation is sending, unsent drafts that nobody edited are updated before they go out.
- An instruction you type in the draft editor takes priority over the prompt. The prompt takes priority over the site's writing instructions in Knowledge.

In **Write emails**, a preview written before the prompt changed shows **Rewrite with this prompt**. Rewriting replaces the preview, including your edits. If the rewrite fails, your current version is kept.

<Warning>
  Do not put facts, prices, or claims in the writing prompt. Add them to [Knowledge](/find-prospects/knowledge) so they are used accurately.
</Warning>

## Link for replies

A link for replies is one page the AI can offer when a lead replies, such as a pricing page or a case study.

1. Enter the page under **Link**.
2. Optionally describe it under **What is this page? (optional)**, in up to 200 characters without links.
3. Select **Check & save**. Use **Remove** to clear it.

Scouty accepts plain `https` pages only. It refuses shorteners, redirects, downloads, scheduling links, and pages flagged as unsafe. Use [Meeting setup](/meetings/google-calendar) for a scheduling link.

The link is added to an AI-suggested reply only when it fits what the lead wrote. It is never added to first emails, follow-ups, or answers to a "no", an unsubscribe request, or an away message. After it has been sent once in a conversation, it is not offered again on its own.

The link is available for saved campaigns.

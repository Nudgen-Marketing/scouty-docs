Scouty writes one personal opening email for each selected contact. Once a campaign is saved, [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation) sends those emails for you. You can also review, edit, and send a draft yourself.

## Save a preview

You can review and edit email previews before signing in. An unsaved campaign shows a limited preview, and locked rows say **Create campaign to view more** or **Login to view more**.

Select **Start campaign**, sign in if needed, and review **Save your campaign**. The first time, **Confirm website and save** also links the workspace to the website. Later campaigns use **Save & start campaign**.

<Warning>
  Saving starts automatic research and sending once your mailbox is ready. It uses your trial credit, then pay-as-you-go at $0.03 per sent email. You can pause the campaign or change its sending hours afterward.
</Warning>

## Choose a sending address

In **Write emails**, enter the address name and **Sender name**. Scouty shows the full hosted address before you confirm it. Choose a name that represents your company because the address is reused for its mailbox.

You can also use a company-owned address after setting up a [Custom domain](/settings/custom-domain). One custom domain is shared across the workspace.

The sending mailbox is shared by everyone in the workspace. An owner or admin sets it up.

## Review the draft

The editor shows **From**, **To**, optional **Cc**, verification state, **Subject**, and **Message**. Select **Add Cc** beside **To** to include colleagues. The optional field stays open when it contains addresses or unfinished input. **Hide Cc** is available only when it is empty. Type addresses or paste a list separated by commas, semicolons, or line breaks. Each address can be removed individually. Scouty allows ten people in total across To and Cc, so an opening email with one To address can include nine Cc addresses. Check every recipient, factual claim, link, and call to action.

To edit directly:

1. Change the subject or message.
2. Select **Save changes**.
3. Use **Discard** if you want to restore the last saved version.

To use AI, open **Help me write**. Choose a quick edit such as **Shorter**, **More specific**, or **Warmer tone**, or describe the change under **Describe your message**, then select **Generate**. Your edits are saved before generating, and the result remains editable.

A campaign's [writing prompt](/email/writing-prompt) and the site's [Knowledge](/find-prospects/knowledge) guide every draft. Your instruction in the editor takes priority.

If personalization fails, Scouty keeps a starting version and displays the AI error. Edit that version yourself or generate again. Nothing is sent because generation failed.

Cc is saved for this outreach sequence only. The opening email and its automatic follow-ups use the same Cc list. It does not change other outreach or email that has already been sent.

## Offer a way to book a meeting

Opening emails do not include a scheduling link. When a lead shows clear meeting interest, Scouty can share your booking link or book through Google Calendar. Set this up once for the site in [Meeting setup](/meetings/google-calendar).

## Send the email

Automation sends drafts for you. To send one yourself, open **Drafts** in the Dashboard sidebar and select the draft. The contact must have a verified address. Save all edits first.

Select **Send email**, review To, Cc, and the subject, then select **Send now**. The message cannot be edited or recalled after it is submitted.

While Scouty is sending or checking the result, wait for the status to update instead of sending the same message again.

If sending is refused because trial credit is used up or a payment needs attention, see [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing). After billing is resolved, return to the draft and send it again.

## Understand send status

| State | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Draft** | The message is saved but has not been submitted. | Continue editing or send it. |
| **Sending…** | The email is still being sent. | Do not send a replacement. |
| **Confirming delivery** or **Delivery not confirmed** | Scouty has not confirmed the result yet. | Select **Check status** and wait for the status to update. Do not send it again. |
| **Sent** | The send is confirmed. | Manage replies in the Mailbox. |
| **Delivered** | Delivery was confirmed for at least one recipient, not necessarily every To/Cc address. | Wait for a reply and monitor the conversation. |
| **Bounced** or **Not delivered** | The email could not be delivered. | Stop using that address and choose another decision maker. |

## See what happened after sending

New emails include tracking automatically. Open [Email analytics](/email/analytics) to see confirmed delivery results and estimated engagement. An open or click does not prove the recipient read the message.

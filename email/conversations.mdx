The **Mailbox** in the Dashboard sidebar holds every email for the workspace, across all campaigns. Everyone in the workspace shares it.

| Folder | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Conversations with replies. Review prepared replies, make them yours, then send. The count shows conversations that need a reply. |
| **Drafts** | First-email drafts waiting for review. |
| **Sent** | Sent conversations and their delivery status. |

The Dashboard opens on **Inbox**.

## Find a conversation

- Search by person, company, or subject.
- Use **Filter by campaign** to show one campaign. The default is **All campaigns**.
- Sort by **Latest activity**, **Latest reply**, or **Oldest first**.
- In **Inbox**, filter with **Needs reply**, **All emails**, **Needs attention**, or **Reply received**.

Select **Check for replies** when you want the latest status. Scouty also updates conversations as new activity arrives.

## Read a conversation

Select an email to open it beside the list. You see:

- the opening email and its delivery status;
- incoming replies in time order;
- the **From**, **To**, and **Cc** recipients on each message;
- automatic follow-ups that were sent, and the follow-up status;
- scheduling activity and meeting status.

A reply from the prospect or a Cc participant stops automatic follow-up. A Cc reply does not increase the prospect reply count or start meeting booking. An out-of-office reply also stops the sequence.

Select **Mark as done** when a conversation needs nothing more. Select **Reopen** to bring it back.

## Read replies in another language

Scouty writes each opening email in the language the recipient most likely reads. When a conversation is not in English, use **Show in English** to read a translation and **Show original** to switch back. Translated messages are labeled **Translated from** and the language. Long messages may be shortened in translation.

Under an AI reply draft in another language, Scouty shows an English translation for you. The original text is what is sent.

## Reply from Scouty

When a prospect replies, Scouty prepares a suggested reply for your review. Suggested replies are not sent automatically.

1. Open the conversation and choose a message.
2. Select **Reply** to answer only its sender, or **Reply all** to include the other To and Cc participants. Scouty removes your own sending address and duplicate recipients.
3. Review and edit To, Cc, and the message. Select **Add Cc** if needed. Paste multiple addresses separated by commas, semicolons, or line breaks. The limit is ten people across To and Cc. Use **Draft with AI** to write a reply to the selected message.
4. Select **Send reply** only when the content and recipients are ready.

A suggested reply can include the campaign's [link for replies](/email/writing-prompt#link-for-replies) or your [meeting setup](/meetings/google-calendar) when it fits what the lead wrote. Replies are free. See [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing).

Closing the panel during the current page visit preserves an unsent reply for that conversation, but send or copy important work before leaving the page.

## Forward one email

Use **Forward** on an individual message when an owner, manager, or colleague needs a copy.

1. Enter up to ten new recipients in **To**. Scouty does not reuse the prospect or old Cc list. Everyone in To can see the other recipients.
2. Add an optional note.
3. Review the full original message and choose which available attachments to include.
4. Select **Send forward**.

If an attachment cannot be loaded, Scouty asks you to review the forward and remove that file before sending. A forward appears separately in the conversation. It does not count as prospect activity and does not stop the prospect's automatic follow-up sequence. Replies in this private exchange stay separate from the prospect conversation.

If a message shows **Sending…** or **Delivery not confirmed**, select **Check status**. You can close and reopen the conversation to check later. Do not create a replacement send while confirmation is pending. **Sent** confirms the send. It does not guarantee that the recipient has read the message.

If your sending address or the original email changes before you send, review the updated message again. Use **Refresh preview** when forwarding.

## When a conversation needs attention

Use the **Needs attention** filter, and look for **Needs you**, when Scouty could not safely complete an action. Examples include a proposed time that needs review, a drafted scheduling response, or an uncertain send result.

If a prospect is not interested, asks you to stop, bounces, or is already handled, stop further outreach and follow the status shown in the conversation. A stop request must always be honored.

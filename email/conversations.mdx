Every sent email remains attached to its project. Open the Dashboard, choose a project, and use the email list to search or filter conversations.

## Project overview

The project shows sent and replied totals, reply rate, meetings, items that need attention, upcoming meetings, and recent conversation activity. Delivery and meeting changes can arrive after the initial send.

Select **Check for replies** when you want the latest conversation status. Scouty also updates the conversation when new activity becomes available.

## Read a conversation

Open an email to see:

- the opening email and its delivery status;
- incoming replies in time order;
- the **From**, **To**, and **Cc** recipients on each message;
- approved automatic follow-ups that were sent;
- scheduling activity and meeting status;
- the current follow-up state and stop reason.

A reply from the prospect or a Cc participant stops automatic follow-up for safety. A Cc reply does not increase the prospect reply count or start meeting booking. A reply labeled as out-of-office also stops the sequence.

## Reply from Scouty

Scouty can draft a reply from the latest conversation context. The generated reply remains in the editor for your review.

1. Open the conversation and choose a message.
2. Select **Reply** to answer only its sender, or **Reply all** to include the other To and Cc participants. Scouty removes your own sending address and duplicate recipients.
3. Review and edit To, Cc, and the message. Select **Add Cc** if needed. Paste multiple addresses separated by commas, semicolons, or line breaks. The limit is ten people across To and Cc. You can also use the AI draft action for the selected message.
4. Select **Send reply** only when the content and recipients are ready.

Scouty does not automatically send these conversation replies. Closing the panel during the current page visit preserves an unsent reply for that conversation, but you should send or copy important work before leaving the page.

## Forward one email

Use **Forward** on an individual message when an owner, manager, or colleague needs a copy.

1. Enter up to ten new recipients in **To**. Scouty does not reuse the prospect or old Cc list. Everyone in To can see the other recipients.
2. Add an optional note.
3. Review the full original message and choose which available attachments to include.
4. Select **Send forward**.

If an attachment cannot be loaded, Scouty asks you to review the forward and remove that file before sending. A forward appears separately in the conversation. It does not count as prospect activity and does not stop the prospect's automatic follow-up sequence. Replies in this private exchange stay separate from the prospect conversation.

If a message shows **Sending** or **Delivery not confirmed**, select **Check status**. You can close and reopen the conversation to check later. Do not create a replacement send while confirmation is pending. **Sent** confirms the send; it does not guarantee that the recipient has read the message.

If your sending address or the original email changes before you send, review the updated message again. Use **Refresh preview** when forwarding.

## When a conversation needs attention

Use **Needs you** when Scouty could not safely complete an action. Examples include a proposed time that needs review, a drafted scheduling response, or an uncertain send result.

If a prospect is not interested, asks you to stop, bounces, or is already handled, stop further outreach and follow the status shown in the conversation. A stop request must always be honored.

---
title: "Auto follow-up"
description: "Send up to three automatic follow-ups that stop when the conversation changes."
---

Auto follow-up writes and sends up to three follow-ups when a recipient has not replied. It is off by default for each site and applies only to emails sent after you enable it.

## Enable it for a site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open site settings">
    Select your website in the Dashboard sidebar and find **Auto follow-up** under **Site settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the time zone">
    Enter the site's **Time zone**, such as `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`. Scouty suggests your current time zone. The time zone cannot be edited while follow-ups are on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable follow-ups">
    Select **Enable follow-ups** and confirm with **Enable**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

New emails then receive AI-written follow-ups automatically when there is no reply. No further setup or approval is needed. Emails sent before you enabled the setting do not get follow-ups.

A draft shows **Auto follow-up for this site: On** or **Off**, with **Change in site settings** to return to this setting.

<Info>
  Follow-ups are sent email. Each one counts toward [billing](/settings/billing) like an opening email.
</Info>

## Edits to the opening email

Follow-ups are written for the opening email as it was sent, and they use its Cc list. Finish editing the subject, message, and Cc before you send.

## Schedule rules

- Scouty can send at most three follow-ups after the opening email.
- Each message waits at least 72 hours after the previous confirmed send.
- Messages send Monday through Friday between 09:00 and 17:00 in the site time zone.
- A due time outside that window moves to the next valid working time.
- If sending was unavailable at the scheduled time, Scouty does not send several missed messages at once.

The conversation displays **0/3** through **3/3 sent**, the next scheduled time, the current status, and a reason when the sequence stops.

## Conditions that stop the sequence

Scouty stops pending follow-ups when it detects any of these conditions:

- any prospect or Cc participant reply, including an out-of-office response;
- a bounce, complaint, or request to stop contact;
- a meeting booked for the conversation;
- a manual email sent in the conversation;
- the conversation marked handled;
- auto follow-up turned off for the site;
- the site archived;
- **Stop follow-ups** selected for the sequence;
- all three follow-ups sent.

If Scouty cannot confirm that it is safe to continue, it delays or stops the sequence and shows the reason in the conversation.

<Warning>
  A message that is already being sent cannot be recalled. A reply that arrives after sending begins cannot cancel that message.
</Warning>

## Turn it off or stop one sequence

Select **Turn off follow-ups** in **Site settings** and confirm with **Turn off** to stop all pending sequences for the site. Turning it on again affects only new emails and does not restart old sequences.

Select **Stop follow-ups** inside a conversation to stop only that sequence. It does not restart automatically.

## When the recipient replies

Scouty stops the sequence, prepares a suggested reply for your review, and notifies you. The suggested reply is not sent automatically. See [conversations and replies](/email/conversations).

[Campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation) controls prospecting and opening emails. Auto follow-up is a separate site setting.

A campaign describes one audience and the reason that audience may need what you sell. Scouty suggests campaigns from your company and competitor research, and you can describe your own.

## Choose a campaign

Each campaign card shows an audience, outcome, pain point, and selection criteria. Select the one closest to the conversation you want to start to review its targeting. Suggestions alone are not saved campaigns.

Under **Who we'll search for**, review:

- industries;
- company sizes;
- locations;
- job titles or seniority used later to find decision makers.

Campaign suggestions use the content language in your company profile.

## Describe your own campaign

Use this when no suggestion fits.

1. Select **Describe your own campaign**.
2. Under **Campaign description**, say who to reach and why, in any language, in 20 to 1,500 characters. Links and addresses are removed.
3. Select **Build campaign**. Scouty writes the campaign in the language of your description.
4. Select **Refine** to change it, then **Rewrite campaign**.

Review the targeting and check matches as you would for a suggested campaign. You can edit the campaign's name, summary, and messaging until you save it.

## Edit targeting

Use the campaign editor when an audience is too broad, too narrow, or aimed at the wrong buyer. Keep locations consistent with the market the company actually serves.

Choose **Industry filter** carefully:

- **Company keywords** searches phrases associated with companies. Existing campaigns keep this behavior until you change it.
- **Exact industry** uses exact industry names, such as `pharmaceuticals`. Review existing entries before switching; a phrase such as “pharmaceutical manufacturing” is not automatically converted.

Choose **Location applies to**:

- **Company headquarters** finds companies headquartered in the selected territory.
- **Contact location** finds people in the selected territory, including people whose employer is headquartered elsewhere. Use exact industries without optional company keywords for this mode.

For pharma prospects in Hanoi, use `pharmaceuticals` and **Hanoi, Vietnam**. Adding **Vietnam** alongside Hanoi expands the search to the country. Do not add biotechnology or medical devices unless you want those audiences too.

Company names can be ambiguous. Scouty omits an employer when it cannot resolve the name to one matching company.

After changing targeting, review the match count and company results again so they reflect the audience you now want to reach.

## Check matches

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the filters">
    Confirm the industries, locations, sizes, and roles under **Who we'll search for**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the preview">
    Select **Check matches**. Scouty displays the provider match count and may show example companies. **Contact location** counts matching people at potential employers; it does not claim that every person represents a different company.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to the result">
    If nothing matches, add an industry or location, or allow more company sizes. If the search is very broad, narrow the industries or locations so better fits are easier to find.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Select **Next step** when the preview represents the audience you want. Scouty then finds companies and contacts for the campaign.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you edit targeting after a preview, the old count is crossed out and Scouty asks you to **Check again**. Run the check before continuing.

## Research allowance messages

If Scouty says your share of today's research allowance is used, new research waits for the next daily allowance. You can continue reviewing results that are already saved. The message, or the **Progress** tab of a saved campaign, shows when research continues.

## Save and reopen a campaign

After reviewing email previews, use **Start campaign** and confirm your website if requested. Saving starts [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation).

The Dashboard sidebar lists saved campaigns under your website. Select one to open it, or select **New campaign** to start another audience for the same site. On a saved campaign, **Edit targeting** changes who is searched for, and automation follows the new targeting. See [your website and campaigns](/getting-started/accounts-and-projects).

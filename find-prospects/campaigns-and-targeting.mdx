A campaign describes one audience and the reason that audience may need what you sell. Scouty suggests campaigns from your company and competitor research.

## Choose a campaign

Each campaign card shows an audience, outcome, pain point, and selection criteria. Choose the one closest to the conversation you want to start.

Under **Who we'll search for**, review:

- industries;
- company sizes;
- locations;
- job titles or seniority used later to find decision makers.

Campaign content follows the content language in your company profile. Filter values remain in the normalized format required by the search provider.

## Edit targeting

Use the campaign editor when an audience is too broad, too narrow, or aimed at the wrong buyer. Keep locations consistent with the market the company actually serves.

Changing targeting creates a new campaign version. Company and contact results from the older version stay separate and are not mixed into the new search.

## Check matches

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the filters">
    Confirm the industries, locations, sizes, and roles under **Who we'll search for**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the preview">
    Select **Check matches**. Scouty displays the matching company count and may show examples.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Respond to the result">
    If nothing matches, add an industry or location, or allow more company sizes. If the search is very broad, narrow the industries or locations so better fits are easier to find.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue">
    Select **Use this campaign** when the preview represents the audience you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you edit targeting after a preview, the old count is crossed out and Scouty asks you to **Check again**. Run the check before continuing.

## Search limit message

If you see “You've reached today's search limit,” Scouty's current search allowance has been used. Cached and saved results can still be available, but a new provider search must wait for the reset.

Search allowance resets at **00:00 UTC**. Convert midnight UTC to your local time and account for daylight-saving changes where applicable. For example, in Vietnam it resets at 07:00 because Vietnam is UTC+7.

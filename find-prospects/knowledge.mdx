**Knowledge** holds the facts and writing preferences Scouty uses when it writes email for your business. Enabled items apply across all of the site's campaigns.

Select **Knowledge** under your website in the Dashboard sidebar. Owners and admins can edit it. Members can view it.

## References

References supply facts, such as product details, pricing, case studies, or answers to common questions.

- Upload a TXT, PDF, or DOCX file of up to 10 MB. Only text is read. Scanned pages and images are not.
- Or select **Add note** and type the information.

A new reference is enabled when it is ready. Use the switch on each item to set it **In use** or **Off**. You can view a reference, edit a note, replace a file, retry a failed upload, or delete the item. A site can hold up to 50 references.

## Writing instructions

Instructions guide how Scouty writes: tone, preferred terms, and claims to avoid.

1. Open **Writing instructions**.
2. Select **Add instruction**, or start from an example such as **Natural tone**, **Preferred terminology**, or **Claims to avoid**.
3. Save it and leave it enabled.

Enabled instructions accompany every AI writing request. A site can hold up to 20 instructions, with 8,000 characters in total.

## How Scouty uses it

- References supply relevant facts. Instructions guide wording.
- Campaign-specific facts take priority over references.
- A campaign's [writing prompt](/email/writing-prompt) takes priority over these writing instructions for that campaign.
- Saved drafts change only when you rewrite them. A draft offers **Rewrite with latest knowledge** when newer knowledge is available.

<Tip>
  Put facts, prices, and claims here. Put tone, length, and structure for one campaign in that campaign's writing prompt.
</Tip>

## Archived sites

Knowledge for an archived site is read-only. Restore the site to edit it.

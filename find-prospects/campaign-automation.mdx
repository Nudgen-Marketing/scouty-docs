---
title: "Campaign automation"
description: "Understand what a saved campaign does on its own, and how to schedule, pause, and monitor it."
---

A saved campaign runs on its own. It keeps finding companies and contacts, writes a personal email for each contact, and sends it. There is no separate start button.

<Warning>
  Saving a campaign starts automatic research and sending once your sending mailbox is ready. Review the audience, the email previews, and your [writing prompt](/email/writing-prompt) before you save.
</Warning>

## What starts when you save

1. Scouty searches for companies that match the campaign's targeting.
2. It finds up to five contacts per company and looks up email addresses when needed.
3. It writes an opening email for each contact in automatic outreach.
4. It sends each email during the campaign's sending hours.

Sent email uses your trial credit, then pay-as-you-go. See [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing).

If the workspace has no sending mailbox yet, Scouty asks an owner or admin to create one. The campaign starts when the mailbox is ready.

## Open a campaign

Select the campaign under **Campaigns** in the Dashboard sidebar. The campaign page has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaign** | The audience, the [writing prompt](/email/writing-prompt), and the link for replies. |
| **Leads** | Companies and people found for the campaign, with each person's progress. |
| **Progress** | Research capacity, totals, and recent activity. |

Use **Open research** to return to the research steps for the campaign, and **All campaigns** to go back to the site.

## Read the status

The campaign header shows **Auto-send** and a status.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Starting**, **Processing**, **Scheduled** | Automation is working or has work queued. |
| **Waiting for sending hours** | Emails are ready and will send in the next sending window. |
| **Retrying**, **Delayed** | A step is being tried again. No action is needed yet. |
| **Confirming sends** | Scouty is confirming recent sends before continuing. |
| **Paused** | Someone paused the campaign. Select **Resume** to continue. |
| **Resume required** | The campaign is ready to continue after a change. Select **Resume**. |
| **Waiting for billing** | Sending needs trial credit or a payment method. See [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing). |
| **Needs attention** | Automation stopped on a problem. Read the reason on the **Progress** tab. |
| **Off (drafts only)** | The workspace prepares drafts and does not send automatically. |
| **Not running** | Nothing is scheduled for the campaign. |

The **Progress** tab's **Activity** list explains what happened and when work resumes. When a daily research limit is reached, the message gives the time and time zone at which it continues. Contacts already queued keep moving.

## Set sending hours

Open **Sending schedule** on the campaign page. An owner or admin can change it.

1. Tick the days email may be sent. Choose at least one.
2. Enter the **Timezone**.
3. Choose **Send from** and **Send until**. The end must be later than the start.
4. Select **Save schedule**.

The schedule applies to new emails and follow-ups. Research may continue outside these hours. A new campaign can send on any day at any hour until you change this.

## Pause and resume

Select **Pause** in the campaign header to stop new work. An action already underway may still finish. Select **Resume** to continue.

Pausing one campaign does not pause the site's other campaigns. To stop all billable sending for the workspace, the owner can use **Stop paid sending** in [Billing & Usage](/settings/billing).

## Who automation contacts

On **Leads → People**, **Match** shows how each person fits the campaign.

| Match | Automatic outreach |
| --- | --- |
| **Buyer persona** | Included. |
| **Related role** or **Other senior** | Shown as **Not in automatic outreach**. Add their job title in **Edit targeting** to include that role. |

**Progress** shows where each person is: **Contact found**, **Review draft**, **View conversation**, or **Reply received**. **May have left this company** warns you to check the person before contacting them.

<Note>
  The **Social** and **Phone** columns show a starred-out placeholder. Scouty does not reveal social profiles or phone numbers.
</Note>

## Drafts-only workspaces

Scouty can set a workspace to prepare drafts without sending. The campaign then shows "Automatic sending is off for this workspace."

An owner or admin can change this on the site page under **Site settings → Automatic sending**:

- **Allow automatic sending** turns sending on. Drafts Scouty already prepared that nobody edited are sent too. Edited drafts stay for you to review.
- **Turn off automatic sending** returns to drafts only.

While sending is off, review and send drafts yourself from **Drafts** in the sidebar. See [draft and send email](/email/drafts-and-sending).

## Archived sites

Archived sites are read-only and their automation stops. After you restore a site, automation stays off. Open each campaign and select **Resume** when you want it to continue.

## Related settings

- [Auto follow-up](/email/auto-follow-up) sends up to three follow-ups when there is no reply. It is a site setting.
- [Meeting setup](/meetings/google-calendar) controls how prospects book time with you.
- [Knowledge](/find-prospects/knowledge) gives Scouty facts and writing preferences for every campaign.

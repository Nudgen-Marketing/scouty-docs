Scouty uses your company profile as the source for every later campaign, fit score, and email. Review it before continuing.

## Start from a website

Enter a company website in Scouty. The research process reads the page language, useful metadata, main content, and market evidence from relevant pages.

The result includes:

- company name, category, and description;
- short product or positioning signals;
- **Language**, used for research and campaign content;
- **Market**, used to find relevant competitors and default prospect locations.

Scouty identifies language and market separately. A `.vn` domain, Vietnamese address, currency, or “Made in Vietnam” statement can support market detection, but it does not prove that the company sells only in Vietnam. A Vietnamese site can also serve a global market.

## Start from the business form

Use the form when you do not have a website. Scouty uses the language of your answers for content and the value you enter in **Markets** for market targeting.

Give a complete description of what you sell and who buys it. Specific information produces better campaigns than broad phrases such as “business services.”

## Correct the profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the editor">
    Select **Not quite right? Edit the details**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the business details">
    Correct **Company name**, **Category**, the description, and key facts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose language and market">
    Choose **Content language**. Then choose a specific market or **Global**.

    If Scouty could not determine the market, you must choose one before **Explore competitors** becomes available.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Save the profile. Scouty marks it **Edited by you** and rebuilds later dependent research when needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Changing the company profile can clear competitors, campaigns, companies, contacts, and unsent drafts that were derived from the old profile. Sent email and saved project history remain unchanged.
</Warning>

## Explore competitors

Select **Explore competitors** after the market is set. Scouty returns up to five direct competitors with evidence that they serve that market. An international brand can appear when there is evidence that it operates in the selected market.

Scouty may return fewer results when it cannot verify market relevance. It does not silently expand a specific local search to unrelated worldwide competitors.

If a result is wrong, go back to the profile and make the market, category, description, or signals more specific.

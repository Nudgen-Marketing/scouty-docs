---
title: "Companies and contacts"
description: "Browse company pages, review role matches, and choose contacts for email drafts."
---

After confirming targeting and selecting **Find contacts**, use **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Write emails** to review results. Contact research can continue while you review a draft.

## Browse companies

The table shows company identity, domain, why it matches, discovered people, and research status. Review the available evidence before contacting a company.

Use **Previous** and **Next** below the table. A display page holds up to 100 companies. Moving to an already loaded page reuses its results. **Next** loads more results only when needed; Scouty does not load every company in advance.

**More available** means another source page may be loaded. Once all available results are loaded, **Page X of Y** reflects the actual list. Provider matches are not a guaranteed number of companies or pages: duplicates and unsuitable results may be excluded. Up to three source pages can be loaded for one campaign.

A source page may add fewer companies than expected or no new companies. The current table remains available. If loading fails, use the retry action shown; existing company, contact, and draft results remain available.

## Review contacts

Scouty keeps up to ten discovered contacts per company and labels their role match:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Target role** | Matches the campaign's intended buyer roles. |
| **Related role** | A related role that may be relevant. |
| **Other role** | Review the person yourself before choosing them. |

Scouty automatically selects up to five contacts across the campaign, choosing the best available target or related match at each company. It does not automatically select every person with an available email. You can select **Draft email** for additional contacts, including another person at the same company or someone labeled **Other role**.

**Available** means the source reports an email may be available. Scouty must find a usable address before preparing the email. Revealing an address can use your search allowance.

## Follow progress

A selected contact can show **Queued**, **Finding email**, **Writing queued**, **Preview ready**, or **Draft ready**. If there is no usable address or the person has left the company, choose another contact. Use **Retry** for a failed item when offered. A quota pause shows when you can continue.

New results do not replace the email you are reviewing. Review each message before sending. An unverified address cannot be sent to.

Company results are saved as you load them. Open the same saved campaign to continue with valid results. For a different audience, create or select another campaign and review its targeting.

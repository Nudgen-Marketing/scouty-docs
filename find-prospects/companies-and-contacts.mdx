title: "Companies and contacts"
description: "Browse company pages, review role matches, and choose contacts for email drafts."
---

After confirming targeting and selecting **Next step**, use **Companies**, **Contacts**, and **Emails** to review results. Contact research can continue while you review a draft.

## Browse companies

The table shows company identity, domain, why it matches, discovered people, and research status. Review the available evidence before contacting a company.

Use the previous and next controls below the table. Moving to an already loaded page reuses its results. More results load only when needed; Scouty does not load every company in advance.

The match count is not a guaranteed number of companies: duplicates and unsuitable results may be excluded. Scouty does not impose a three-page cap. The search display limit and your research allowance still apply.

Before you save a campaign, the preview shows a limited number of companies, contacts, and emails. Locked rows read **Create campaign to view more** or **Login to view more**.

In **Contact location** mode, the provider total counts people, not unique companies. Several people can belong to the same company.

A source page may add fewer companies than expected or no new companies. The current table remains available. If loading fails, use the retry action shown; existing company, contact, and draft results remain available.

If the search display limit is reached, narrow the targeting to explore a specific segment. If the source repeats a page, Scouty keeps your results and pauses further loading. A search with an unconfirmed outcome does not start another lookup automatically. Check the displayed status before retrying.

## Review contacts

Scouty keeps up to five contacts per company. Email addresses are looked up when needed, and saved results are reused. It labels their role match:

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Target role** | Matches the campaign's intended buyer roles. |
| **Related role** | A related role that may be relevant. |
| **Other role** | Review the person yourself before choosing them. |

Scouty automatically chooses contacts for the first emails, preferring the best available target or related match at each company. It does not automatically select every person with an available email. You can select **Draft email** for additional contacts, including another person at the same company or someone labeled **Other role**.

A saved campaign's **Leads → People** table uses the labels **Buyer persona**, **Related role**, and **Other senior**, and shows who is in automatic outreach. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation#who-automation-contacts).

<Note>
  The **Social** and **Phone** columns show a starred-out placeholder. Scouty does not reveal social profiles or phone numbers.
</Note>

**Available** means the source reports an email may be available. Scouty must find a usable address before preparing the email. Revealing an address can use your research allowance.

## Follow progress

A selected contact can show **Queued**, **Finding email**, **Writing queued**, **Preview ready**, or **Draft ready**. If there is no usable address or the person has left the company, choose another contact. Use **Retry** for a failed item when offered. A quota pause shows when you can continue.

New results do not replace the email you are reviewing. Review the previews before you save: after saving, [automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation) sends emails without asking again, unless the workspace is set to drafts only. An unverified address cannot be sent to.

Company results are saved as you load them. Open the same saved campaign to continue with valid results. For a different audience, create or select another campaign and review its targeting.

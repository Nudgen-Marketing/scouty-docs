A connected app works through tools. Each tool needs one permission, and you approve permissions in Scouty. The app can only use tools covered by the permissions you approved, in the workspace you chose.

## Permissions

| Permission | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| **Read workspace** | Read your campaigns, conversations, and account information. Every new connection starts with this. |
| **Research businesses** | Run business research and competitor discovery, and search for companies and decision makers, using your allowance. |
| **Reveal contacts** | Reveal contact details using your allowance. |
| **Manage campaigns** | Create and update your projects, research, and campaigns. |
| **Draft outreach** | Generate and edit outreach drafts. Does not send. |
| **Send messages** | Send, reply, and forward messages without asking Scouty again for each message. |
| **Automate follow-ups** | Configure automatic follow-up messages without asking Scouty again for each send. |
| **Manage meetings** | Read calendar availability and book meetings. |

Ask the app for the smallest set of permissions your task needs. You can see what each connected app holds in [Connected Apps](/settings/connected-apps).

<Info>
  Your workspace role still applies. For example, only an owner or admin can confirm the workspace website, whatever the app was granted.
</Info>

## Actions with lasting effects

<Warning>
  These actions cannot be undone from the app. Ask the app to show you the details and confirm before it runs them.
</Warning>

- **Confirming a website** permanently links the workspace to that website. Another website cannot replace it. Researching a website does not confirm it.
- **Creating a campaign** starts automatic research and sending for that campaign once the mailbox is ready. There is no per-campaign cap on emails, and sent email is billed to the workspace. If your workspace is set to drafts only, automation prepares drafts until an owner or admin allows automatic sending in Scouty. An app cannot change that setting. See [campaign automation](/find-prospects/campaign-automation).
- **Sending, replying, and forwarding** deliver real email that cannot be recalled.
- **Turning on follow-ups** authorizes Scouty to write and send up to three follow-ups for every email sent afterward, with no separate approval. See [Auto follow-up](/email/auto-follow-up).
- **Booking a meeting** creates a calendar event and sends an invitation.

## Safeguards around sending

Scouty applies the same rules to an app as to you in the Dashboard.

- **Reviewed drafts only.** The app sends a draft by the version it last read. If the draft changed since, Scouty rejects the send and the app must read it again.
- **No free-typed recipients for outreach.** Opening emails, replies, and bookings use the recipients saved on the outreach. The app cannot substitute an email address. Forwards are the exception: you name their recipients.
- **No duplicate sends.** If the result of a send or booking is unknown, Scouty will not run it again. Check the email or meeting in Scouty before you ask the app to do anything further.
- **Follow-ups match the reviewed email.** A follow-up sequence belongs to the opening email as it was read. Editing that email afterward means the app must prepare the sequence again.
- **Calendar sign-in stays in the browser.** An app cannot connect Google Calendar for you. It gives you the Scouty link to finish in your browser. See [Google Calendar and meetings](/meetings/google-calendar).
- **Meeting times are checked.** A meeting must start at least 10 minutes from now and within 90 days, and its length must match the project's **Meeting length**.

## What an app cannot do

- Work in a workspace other than the one you approved.
- Manage people, billing, notification settings, or a custom domain.
- Connect or disconnect Google Calendar.
- Allow automatic sending for a workspace that is set to drafts only.
- Save a campaign you described yourself in Scouty. Apps create campaigns from suggestions.
- Bypass a usage limit.

## When a tool fails

Your AI app shows Scouty's message. These are the common ones.

| Message is about | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A missing permission | The connection does not include the permission the tool needs. | Approve it in Scouty when the app asks, or reconnect. |
| Your workspace role | Your role does not allow the action. | Ask an owner or admin. |
| Invalid tool arguments | The app used out-of-date tool definitions. | Ask the app to refresh Scouty's tools and read the item again. |
| A usage limit | An allowance is used up. The message includes when to retry. | Wait until that time. See [limits](/help/limits-and-troubleshooting). |
| A changed draft or campaign | Someone edited it after the app read it. | Ask the app to read the latest version, then review it again. |
| An action in progress or unknown outcome | Scouty could not confirm a send or booking. | Check its status in Scouty. Do not retry it. |
| A result that is too large | The action finished but the result could not be returned. | Ask for a smaller page or open Scouty. Do not repeat the action. |
| Scouty temporarily unavailable | A temporary problem. | For anything that sends or books, check its status in Scouty before retrying. |

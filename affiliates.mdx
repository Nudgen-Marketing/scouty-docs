Introduce businesses to Scouty and earn **10% of all paid Scouty revenue** from the customers you refer. You continue earning for as long as those customers keep paying.

[**Book a partner call**](https://cal.com/toannhu/30min) to discuss your audience and agree on your referral setup.

## Who the program is for

The program is for agencies, consultants, creators, and community owners who work with businesses that need prospecting and outreach. Scouty helps those businesses find relevant prospects, write personal email, and turn replies into meetings.

## How revenue share works

| Program detail | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue share | You earn 10% of all paid Scouty revenue from your referred customers. |
| Lifetime | You continue earning for as long as your referred customers keep paying. This is an ongoing revenue share, not a one-time reward. |
| Included payments | All paid Scouty revenue is included, including usage and credit purchases. The share is not limited to subscription payments. |
| Referral setup and payouts | You discuss these details on your partner call before you start referring customers. |

For example, **\$1,000** in paid Scouty revenue from referred customers earns you **\$100** in commission.

<Note>
This is an illustrative example. Your actual earnings depend on your referred customers’ paid Scouty revenue. There is no guaranteed earnings amount.
</Note>

## Get started

Before your call, think about the clients, audience, or community you want to introduce to Scouty and how prospecting could help them.

1. Select [**Book a partner call**](https://cal.com/toannhu/30min) and choose an available time.
2. Tell us about the people or businesses you want to refer. We will discuss whether Scouty fits their needs.
3. Agree on your referral setup and review payout details together before making referrals.

The call is your starting point for understanding how the partnership will work. If you cannot find a suitable time, contact [contact@scouty.to](mailto:contact@scouty.to) for help arranging a conversation.
